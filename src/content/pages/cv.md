### Founder — DATAPULSE, Greece · 2022 – 2024

*B2B analytics product for Greek retail pharmacies.*

- Identified an operational problem in my own industry and built a Power BI analytics product directly on the Farmakon ERP SQL database — the system I operate daily, giving domain access no outside vendor had.
- Built the product solo over roughly a year (2022–23), on the SQL and Power BI training completed the year before, using ChatGPT as a build partner in the first months it existed.
- Registered the business and produced the whole commercial package alone — naming, business cards, flyers and their copy, value proposition, target list and Athens visiting plan — and launched to market at the end of 2023.
- Built a technically filtered target list (pharmacies known to run the Farmakon ERP), hired and directed a salesperson to run door-to-door discovery in Athens, and ran doors and demos myself.
- **Funnel: ~90 pharmacies approached → ~10 booked demos → 2 paying customers.**
- Ran the discovery that killed the product: pharmacists did not want metrics, they wanted workload removed — and a large share of them never entered purchase invoices digitally, so the data my product depended on did not exist in their systems.
- Priced it as a one-off installation fee. That made the first yes easier, but it meant no income after the sale, and no renewal moment to tell me whether the two customers who bought it found it worth keeping.
- Closed the business on that evidence in June 2024 rather than spend further into it.
